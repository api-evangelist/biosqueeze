# BioSqueeze

BioSqueeze Inc. is a biomineralization platform company that engineers the subsurface by forming crystalline calcium carbonate in place.

Core capabilities:
- Strengthen soils and unconsolidated materials.
- Seal fluid pathways, fractures, pores, micro-annuli and voids.
- Heal damaged cementitious and critical infrastructure.

Primary markets:
- Energy and oil & gas: well integrity, water shutoff, fines migration and sand control, flood conformance, CO2 containment and refracturing.
- Defense: airfield subgrade stabilization, expeditionary construction and critical-asset reinforcement.
- Mining: groundwater control, ground stabilization, waste sequestration and dust control.
- Infrastructure and environmental systems.
